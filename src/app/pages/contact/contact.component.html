Google Developer Group Siliguri is a community-run meetup for developers interested in resources and technology from Google Developers. We engage student developers, fresh graduates and professionals through our hack events and meetups.

Our motive is to build a local ecosystem of programmers and hackers in and around Siliguri. We organise tech talks, workshops, bootcamps and Google flagship events where programmers meet to discuss and work with Google developer resources and products.
